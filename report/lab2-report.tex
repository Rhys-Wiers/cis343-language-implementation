        {\Large \textbf{Lab 2: AST Printer}}
    \end{center}

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{Grammar:}
            \begin{equation*}
            \begin{array}{lcl}
            \nt{expression} & \rightarrow & \nt{logic\_or} \\[4pt]
            \nt{logic\_or}  & \rightarrow & \nt{logic\_and}\ \left(\,\term{\textbar\textbar}\ \nt{logic\_and}\,\right)* \\[4pt]
            \nt{logic\_and} & \rightarrow & \nt{equality}\ \left(\,\term{\&\&}\ \nt{equality}\,\right)* \\[4pt]
            \nt{equality}   & \rightarrow & \nt{comparison}\ \left(\,(\term{!=} \mid \term{==})\ \nt{comparison}\,\right)* \\[4pt]
            \nt{comparison} & \rightarrow & \nt{term}\ \left(\,(\term{>} \mid \term{>=} \mid \term{<} \mid \term{<=})\ \nt{term}\,\right)* \\[4pt]
            \nt{term}       & \rightarrow & \nt{factor}\ \left(\,(\term{-} \mid \term{+})\ \nt{factor}\,\right)* \\[4pt]
            \nt{factor}     & \rightarrow & \nt{unary}\ \left(\,(\term{/} \mid \term{*} \mid \term{\%})\ \nt{unary}\,\right)* \\[4pt]
            \nt{unary}      & \rightarrow & (\term{!} \mid \term{-})\ \nt{unary} \mid \nt{primary} \\[4pt]
            \nt{primary}    & \rightarrow & \texttt{NUMBER} \mid \texttt{STRING} \mid \term{true} \mid \term{false} \mid \term{nil} \\
                            &             & \mid\ \term{(}\ \nt{expression}\ \term{)}
            \end{array}
            \end{equation*}

            Since my language uses ``\&\&" and ``||", I have included those in the grammar above. In addition, I have included ``\%".
        \item \textbf{Printer Testing:}
            From the project root, you can run the following to compile the printer and the tests:
            \begin{minted}{bash}
g++ -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra src/token.cpp \
src/ast-printer.cpp test/lab2/ast-test.cpp \
-o ast-test
            \end{minted}

            Then you can run the executable:
            \begin{minted}{bash}
./ast-test
            \end{minted}

        \item \textbf{Test Cases:}
            The test cases cover all literal values (numbers, strings, booleans, nil), all the unary operators, binary operators, comparisons, ``\&\&" and ``||", and larger arithmetic and logic expressions that use multiple different operators. In the interest of time, I used AI to generate the test cases.
